FileScape Windows playtest
=========================

FileScape turns folders into little worlds you can explore on foot.

1. Extract the whole ZIP and keep its files together.
2. Open FileScape.exe. The game starts in Planets at This computer.
3. Choose Learn to play for a guided tour of Starter Sky, the bundled sample world.
   You can reopen the tour from Help > Learn to play.

Left-drag to look around, use WASD to move, and press Space to jump.
Stand on a launcher and press Space to fly to its folder.
Press T for Overview, Esc to return to the surface, and F1 for all controls.

From Command Prompt, FileScape.exe returns to the prompt at once. To watch the log
in the window you started it from, run FileScape.console.exe instead: it returns
when you close the game.

If you report a problem, include the build ID from build-info.txt and the steps
that got you there. Starter Sky is a good place to capture a screenshot you
are comfortable sharing.

Third-party licences and notices are in the licenses folder.
